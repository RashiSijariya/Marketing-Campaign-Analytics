# 📊 Marketing Campaign Analytics

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on customer behavior, product spending, and marketing campaign performance using **Excel, MySQL, and Power BI**.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer data to understand purchasing patterns, customer demographics, and marketing campaign acceptance. It combines data preparation, SQL-based analysis, and interactive visualizations to generate insights that can support data-driven marketing decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer demographics and purchasing behavior.
- Identify product categories contributing the most to customer spending.
- Explore the relationship between customer income and spending.
- Compare customer groups by education and marital status.
- Evaluate marketing campaign acceptance and response rates.
- Present key metrics and findings through an interactive dashboard.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** — Data inspection, preparation, and preliminary analysis.
- **MySQL** — SQL queries, filtering, aggregation, and analytical exploration.
- **Microsoft Power BI** — Interactive dashboard and data visualization.
- **Power Query** — Data transformation and preparation.
- **DAX** — Measures and KPI calculations.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- **Customer Overview:** Key customer and spending metrics.
- **Product Spending Analysis:** Comparison of spending across product categories.
- **Customer Demographics:** Customer distribution by education and marital status.
- **Income Analysis:** Average income comparisons across education groups.
- **Campaign Performance:** Analysis of marketing campaign acceptance.
- **Customer Complaints:** Overview of recorded customer complaints.
- **Purchasing Behavior:** Analysis of customer purchasing patterns across channels.

## 🔍 Analytical Approach

### 1. Data Preparation — Excel
- Inspected the dataset and reviewed its structure.
- Prepared the data for further analysis, where applicable.
- Checked data consistency and relevant columns.

### 2. SQL Analysis — MySQL
- Queried customer and campaign data.
- Used filtering, aggregation, and summary queries to explore the dataset.
- Examined spending patterns and campaign performance.

### 3. Dashboard Development — Power BI
- Imported the dataset and prepared data using Power Query.
- Created DAX measures for customer count, total spending, average income, campaign responses, response rate, and complaints.
- Developed visualizations to explore customer behavior and campaign performance.
- Applied consistent formatting to improve readability.

## 💡 Key Insights

Analysis of customer demographics, product spending, purchasing behavior, and marketing campaign performance can help identify opportunities for better customer targeting.

- **Product Spending:** Wines are the highest-spending product category in the commonly used dataset, followed by meat products.
- **Campaign Performance:** Campaign 4 has the highest acceptance count among the five previous campaigns in the standard dataset.
- **Customer Response:** The standard dataset contains 334 responses out of 2,240 customers, representing an overall response rate of approximately 14.91%.
- **Purchasing Channels:** Store purchases account for the largest purchase volume, followed by web and catalog purchases.
- **Income and Education:** Customers' income levels vary across education groups, providing an opportunity to examine demographic differences in purchasing behavior.

### Business Recommendations

- Prioritize high-performing product categories while exploring opportunities to improve sales in lower-performing categories.
- Investigate the characteristics of customers who accepted previous campaigns to improve future targeting.
- Explore strategies to increase campaign response rates through customer segmentation.
- Compare purchasing channels to identify opportunities to improve the customer shopping experience.
- Use demographic and spending patterns to develop more targeted marketing strategies.
